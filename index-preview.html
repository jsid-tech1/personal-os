How it works

You write what is on your mind. The assistant proposes. You decide.

Capture → Understand → Break down → Schedule → Execute → Adapt

  1. 01

    Describe your task

    Write in plain paragraphs about a studio brief, an upcoming deadline, or the hours you have this week. No rigid forms to fill out before you start.

  2. 02

    Make the plan your own

    The assistant breaks the work into clear steps, milestones, and a draft schedule. You approve, edit, or reject each item before anything is saved. Nothing moves silently.

  3. 03

    Finish today's tasks

    Open the planner in the morning to see what belongs to today, and check off what you finish at night to adapt tomorrow and close the loop.
