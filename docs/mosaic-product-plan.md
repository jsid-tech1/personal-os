# Mosaic planning rebuild

Kind: software, a personal task planner for students and independent creative work.

Client-directed identity: retain Personal OS and account records. Milesdane Trial display, DM Mono interface; mist #f3f4f0, ink #202923, forest #315b43, raspberry #c22d63, petal #efb4c8, chartreuse #d5df83. Colour follows the supplied botanical/pixel references. No source poster or photograph is reused. Artwork is original deterministic geometric HTML tiles.

Layout: generous left-aligned type with a mosaic/product composition beside it, stacked on mobile. Navigation: Today, Tasks, Calendar, My Mosaic, Focus and Profile. Historical routes and user data remain available. Copy: plain, calm, actionable; never call a deterministic fallback AI.

Landing: hero, three-step product demonstration, interactive mosaic demonstration and final authentication action. Mosaic interaction follows completion, not a looping animation. Accessibility: visible focus, labelled fields, 44px controls, reduced motion, no colour-only status.

Dealt moment: `word-highlight`, on the mosaic section's statement. Words gradually take ink as the reader reaches the explanation; under reduced motion the statement stands in full ink. The kit files were copied unchanged into assets/moments. The artwork itself responds only to the demonstration button or actual task completion.

Architecture: existing Google authentication; account-local state plus version-checked automatic Firestore snapshots. Authenticated server-only AI endpoint uses an environment secret and structured output. Deterministic scheduling validates capacity before explicit approval. Manual creation remains available without AI. Errors and conflicts are visible, with no silent cross-device overwrite.

Milesdane Trial is personal-use only. Commercial or large-audience release needs the appropriate license: https://ekobimantara.com/product/milesdane/. License retained in assets/fonts/Read Me.txt.
